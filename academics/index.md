## Ph.D.

I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from [The Ohio State University](https://polisci.osu.edu/) in 2014, with a minor in quantitative methods. I was funded by the Fulbright Commission and the Brazilian government.

My dissertation used natural-language processing and 42 million news articles from more than 6,000 sources to construct a machine-coded democracy index covering countries from 1993 to 2012. A shortened version became [Automated Democracy Scores](http://bibliotecadigital.fgv.br/ojs/index.php/bre/article/view/58160), published in the *Brazilian Review of Econometrics*.

## Current research

- [A better 1RM formula](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17495), with large-scale observational data from Fitbod. We evaluated 303,494 near-failure sets from 14,966 users across 388 exercises and derived a formula that was substantially more internally consistent than classical 1RM equations.
- [Predictors of long-term resistance exercise adherence among beginners](https://sportrxiv.org/index.php/server/preprint/view/709/version/894), with Federica Conti, Andy Galpin, and Brad Schoenfeld. The paper uses Fitbod data to study which early behaviors predict whether new lifters continue training over the long run.

## Teaching

I have taught undergraduate, graduate, and professional courses in statistics, data mining, machine learning, deep learning, and data science at IDP, IESB, IPEA, and the University of Brasilia. Several recorded lectures are collected on the [talks and interviews](/talks/) page.

I have also served, occasionally, on undergraduate and graduate thesis committees at FGV, the University of Brasilia, and other institutions.
