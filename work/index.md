## Fitbod

I lead data science, research, and AI at [Fitbod](https://fitbod.me/). The job includes setting the team's technical and research agenda, hiring and developing data scientists, deciding how we evaluate product changes, and working with product and engineering on the company roadmap. I also remain hands-on: I write Python and SQL, design and analyze experiments, inspect models, and prototype new product ideas.

### Recommendation system

I helped rewrite Fitbod's workout recommendation algorithm in Python and move it from the mobile client to a server-side system. I worked on the algorithm itself as well as the architecture and migration plan. Moving it server-side made the system much easier to change and increased our experimentation velocity by roughly eightfold.

Since then, I have led work on exercise selection, workout construction, progression, recovery, and strength estimation. Some of that work became product experiments; some became production models; some led to two patent applications.

### Experimentation

I established the methodology Fitbod uses for product experiments and standardized how teams define outcomes, assign users, handle repeated measurements, and decide whether a result is worth shipping. I design some experiments directly and review many of the experiments run by other teams.

The resulting work has included experiments that increased conversion by as much as 13% and improved measured strength gains by as much as 6%. Just as importantly, it made it possible to test changes to the workout algorithm rather than relying on anecdotes or intuition.

### Applied research

I created Fitbod's research program and work with exercise scientists on studies that use the app's longitudinal data. Current projects include research on long-term resistance-training adherence and on estimating one-repetition maximum from ordinary training sets.

This work sits between academic research and product development: the papers need to survive scientific scrutiny, but the underlying methods also need to be useful inside the product.

## Earlier government work

Before Fitbod, I worked in the Brazilian federal government. I built or helped build machine-learning systems for audit targeting, public-procurement classification, supplier-risk detection, fake-export detection, and government property valuation.

The work was unusually practical. Models had to fit existing legal and operational processes, run on imperfect administrative data, and produce outputs that auditors and public servants could actually use. A few of those projects later became papers, talks, or open-source code, but most were internal systems.
